## Important safeguards
- No automatic publication or claims of validated effectiveness based on metadata or citation count.
- Assessments are human-entered and comparison charts are descriptive counts, not meta-analysis.
- Public catalog only approved records; admin functions require authenticated Supabase account and admin RLS.
- Do not introduce self-modifying production code or autonomous privileged changes without explicit approval.

## Longer-term vision (not yet implemented)
A human-governed, research-institute-like autonomous system started by a single control: discover, retrieve, verify, critically appraise, compare, classify, store, monitor, and propose improvements, with human approval for high-impact actions and an emergency stop.
